Data-to-decision workflow

1. Collect — Organize source extracts, campaign activity, CRM stages and field audit data.
2. Validate — Check record counts, missing fields, data types and conflicting classifications.
3. Transform — Derive rates, weighted or unweighted means as appropriate, comparisons and business KPIs.
4. Visualize — Present trends, exceptions, performance against targets and funnel conversion.
5. Recommend — Translate findings into client service, campaign, operational and recruiting conversations.
